### FAQ

**What is staff augmentation?**  
It is a flexible way to add external specialists to your existing team while keeping product and delivery ownership on your side.

**How quickly can someone start?**  
It depends on the role and interview process. Jelly Drop highlights people whose availability has been recently confirmed.

**How do you verify specialists?**  
We review experience, technical fit, communication, expectations and availability before presenting a profile. The exact technical process can be adjusted to your role.

**Can you build a full team?**  
Yes. We can assemble dedicated or mixed teams across engineering, QA, Cloud/DevOps, Data/AI and eCommerce.

**Where are your specialists based?**  
Our core network is based in Poland and Central Europe and works remotely with clients across Europe and beyond.

**What happens if the match does not work?**  
We agree on a transparent feedback and replacement process before the engagement begins.

**Do you work with software houses?**  
Yes. Software houses are one of our primary partner groups. We can support overflow, niche skills, urgent delivery gaps and longer-term team extension.
