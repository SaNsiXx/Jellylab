## What we use

| Category | What | Stored where | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | your cookie choice (`jl-consent`) | browser localStorage | always |
| Essential | campaign source for forms (`jl-attr`: UTM, referrer, landing page) | browser sessionStorage, cleared when the tab closes | always; sent only with a form you submit |
| Analytics | Google Analytics 4 (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) | cookies | only after you accept analytics |
| Marketing | LinkedIn Insight Tag (`li_*`, `bcookie`, …) | cookies | only after you accept marketing |

Google Consent Mode v2 is set to "denied" by default; nothing non-essential loads before your choice.

Use "Cookie settings" in the footer at any time. [DO UZUPEŁNIENIA — retention of each cookie, vendor details]
