This policy explains how **JLTECHNOLOGY sp. z o.o.** (Jelly Labs), the controller described in the Privacy Policy, uses cookies and similar technologies (browser localStorage and sessionStorage) on jellylabs.tech.

## What we use

| Category | Name | Provider | Stored where | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | `jl-consent` | Jelly Labs | localStorage | remembers your cookie choice | until you clear browser data or we update this policy |
| Essential | `jl-attr` | Jelly Labs | sessionStorage | campaign source (UTM, referrer, landing page), sent only with a form you submit | until the tab is closed |
| Essential | `jl-doi-ok` | Jelly Labs | sessionStorage | shows the right message after you confirm your email | until the tab is closed |
| Analytics | `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Ireland Ltd. (Google Analytics 4) | cookies | anonymous statistics on how the site is used | up to 2 years |
| Marketing | `li_sugr`, `bcookie`, `lidc`, `UserMatchHistory`, `AnalyticsSyncHistory` | LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company (Insight Tag) | cookies | measuring LinkedIn campaigns | from 1 day to 1 year |

Essential storage is needed for the site and forms to work, so it does not require consent (Art. 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law). Analytics and marketing tools load **only after you accept them**; Google Consent Mode v2 is set to "denied" by default, so nothing optional runs before your choice. Accepting and rejecting are equally easy.

Data collected by Google and LinkedIn may be transferred to the USA on the basis described in section 4 of the Privacy Policy. More information: [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [LinkedIn cookie policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/cookie-policy).

## Changing your choice

Use "Cookie settings" in the footer at any time to change or withdraw your consent. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; the site will then ask you again.
