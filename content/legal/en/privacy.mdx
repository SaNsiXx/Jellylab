With every form we also store the submission time (UTC), form language, page and traffic source (UTM parameters, referrer) and the exact text and version of the consents given. **We do not store your IP address with submissions.** For spam protection we only keep an irreversible hash of it for a short time.

Providing data is voluntary, but without the fields marked as required we cannot reply to your request or consider your application. We do not make solely automated decisions, including profiling that produces legal effects.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **Sales and contact enquiries:** for the duration of talks, then until the limitation period for possible claims expires (generally 3 years), or shorter if you successfully object.
- **Newsletter and marketing:** until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe; proof of consent for the limitation period.
- **Unconfirmed sign-ups (double opt-in):** the confirmation link is valid for 7 days; unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted within 30 days.
- **Recruitment for a specific project:** until the process ends, no longer than 6 months from the application.
- **Future recruitment (separate consent):** until you withdraw consent, no longer than 2 years from when it was given.
- **Analytics data:** according to the tools' settings, no longer than 14 months.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (and always to direct marketing). You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal — use the unsubscribe link in an email or write to [office@jellylabs.tech](mailto:office@jellylabs.tech).

We reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest. You may also lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (PUODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland.

## 7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit access to submissions to the people who need them, and keep secrets and access keys on the server side only.

Information about cookies and similar technologies is in our Cookie Policy. When we change this policy we update the date at the top of the page and inform people who gave us consents about significant changes.
