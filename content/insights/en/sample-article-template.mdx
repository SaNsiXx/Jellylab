## What an article looks like

Each article lives in `content/insights/{en,pl}/<slug>.mdx`. The frontmatter defines the title, excerpt, category (`jelly_drop`, `market_signals`, `delivery`, `people`, `ai_technology`), author and dates. Reading time is calculated automatically.

## Writing guidelines

- Short sentences, strong verbs, no empty promises.
- Every figure needs a source you can show.
- Keep the provocative tone for headlines and teasers — never at the expense of clarity.
